Merchant Journal & Ledger

A companion journal is always created and is GM-only. It tracks Storefront info, Inventory/Treasury, Customer Relations, Transaction Ledger, and GM Notes. Players use the merchant storefront instead of receiving the private ledger.

The journal name is Merchant Name — Scene Name; duplicate names receive 2, 3, 4…

Keeping the ledger GM-only prevents transaction history, treasury details, customer favor relationships, and GM notes from leaking to players.

Restocking

The Merchant Manager includes a Restock button. Automatic real-time schedules are intentionally deferred until the base economy survives live play without transforming into accounting gelatin.
